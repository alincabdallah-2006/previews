What we
help with.

Four areas of care, one clinic on Spintex Road. Tap any card to ask about it on WhatsApp.

Spine care

Physiotherapy for neck pain and back pain, starting with an assessment of how you sit, stand and move.

Stroke rehabilitation

Guided, patient rehabilitation sessions working on movement, balance and everyday tasks.

Sports injuries

Care for strains, sprains and overuse problems, with a plan for returning to training.

Therapeutic massage

Hands-on massage as part of your physiotherapy, focused on tight, sore or overworked muscles.
