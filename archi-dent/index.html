The approach

Drawn first.
Then built.

The name says it: Archi Dent treats a smile as a piece of architecture. Lines, proportions and function are considered as one design, led by Dr Tatiana Zougheib.

i.

Listen

Your visit starts with a conversation: what bothers you, what you would like, and what matters most to you.

ii.

Study

A careful look at teeth, gums and bite, so that any recommendation rests on what is actually there.

iii.

Plan

Options are laid out clearly, step by step, so you understand each stage before deciding.

iv.

Craft

Treatment carried out with patience and an eye for detail, at a pace that keeps you comfortable.
