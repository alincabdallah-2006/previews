The finish

Gloss is in
the prep.

A good finish comes from the steps before the shine. This is the usual order of work for paint care.

  • 01

    Wash and decontaminate

    Dirt and bonded grime are removed so the surface is clean to work on.

  • 02

    Polish

    The paint is machine polished to refine the surface and lift the gloss.

  • 03

    Protect

    Ceramic coating, if you choose it, is applied over the prepared paint.
